About RC E-Cell

RC E-Cell is the premier entrepreneurship and innovation community of DSEU Rajokri Campus. We are dedicated to fostering an entrepreneurial mindset among students, equipping them with the right tools, mentorship, and opportunities to turn visionary ideas into reality.

From conducting immersive workshops and seminars to organizing competitive startup challenges, we provide a dynamic launchpad for future founders, innovators, and business leaders.
